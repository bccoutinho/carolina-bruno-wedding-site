Pousada Castelo de Alvito

A 15th-century castle in the Baixo Alentejo, about 150 km south of Lisbon. Guests are welcome starting 18 June.

Pousada Castelo de Alvito
Largo do Castelo, 7920-999 Alvito (Beja), Portugal
Open in Google Maps

In the Alentejo, about 1h30 south-east of Lisbon. Not the Alvito neighbourhood in Lisbon!

Getting there

By car

~1h30 from Lisbon (~150 km). Parking in and around the village.

By train

Metro to Lisboa-Oriente, then CP to Alvito station (about 2h20). The station is a short ride from the castle.

Flying in

Fly to Lisbon (LIS), then drive or train down.

Where to stay

With us

We provide accommodation inside the castle and in nearby pousadas in Alvito.

Nearby

Quinta do Paral (about 15 min); Beja or Évora (about 25 to 40 min).
